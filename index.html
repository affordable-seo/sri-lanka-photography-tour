The Experience

Capture Sri Lanka Through Your Lens

This 13-day Sri Lanka photography tour presents photographers with an extraordinary range of subjects—from the energetic streets and colorful markets of Colombo and Kandy to mist-covered highlands carpeted in emerald tea estates. Discover secluded coastlines, traditional fishermen working tirelessly, ancient temples, thriving wildlife, and sweeping mountain vistas.

The island's rich multicultural tapestry brings vibrant festivals and celebrations throughout the year, creating exceptional photographic moments. With 2026 shaping up to be a remarkable year for travel to Sri Lanka, now is the ideal time to explore beyond the crowded tourist hotspots like Ella's Nine Arch Bridge and uncover hidden gems waiting to be photographed.

Whether your passion lies in documentary street photography, portraiture, landscapes, architectural details, or wildlife, Sri Lanka delivers as an unmatched photography destination.
